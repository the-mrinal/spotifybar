Now Playing for Spotify — four alternatives

You look up from work to see what's playing. That might be on this Mac or on a Spotify Connect device. Each alternative is shown in three states: at rest, hovering and clicked, on both a light and a dark menu bar.

1 CSS px = 1 pt · true scale

SF Pro 13 / NSStatusItem + NSPanel / NSMenu
Track, artist & art are placeholders
A
Quiet Native

Takes up the least space: just art and a title. Controls live in a standard NSMenu, so keyboard navigation, VoiceOver and Esc work for free, the same as in system menus.

≈166 ptNSMenuThis Mac
B
Glass Stage

The hover card does most of the work. It's tinted with the album art and has controls inline, so you can skip a track with one click. Clicking the item just pins the card for keyboard use.

≈200 ptNSPanel + glassEffectKitchen speaker
C
Island

The bar shows no text, only 30 pt of art plus a progress ring you can notice from the corner of your eye. A black island unfolds from the item, like the Dynamic Island, but only when you hover over or click it.

≈30 ptBorderless NSPaneliPhone
D
Stacked Ticker

Title and artist are stacked in a single 24 pt bar, with a device glyph that shows remote playback even at rest. Hovering reveals transport controls in the bar itself. Clicking opens a remote organised around the playback device.
